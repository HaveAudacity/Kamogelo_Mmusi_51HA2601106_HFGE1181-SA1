# Level_Fortress
A top-down eat-or-be-eaten game made in Unity for Foundations of Game Engines 1A  / HFGE1181 SA1 .
You are a donut in a fortress. Eat food and smaller enemies to grow, and avoid anything bigger than you.

## How to play
- Eat food and smaller enemies to fill your satiation. Every 4 bites make you grow.
- You can't eat food that's larger than you.
- A larger enemy pulls you toward its centre. If it reaches your centre, it's game over.
- Enemies that shrink below 25% of your size disappear.
- Arrows around you point toward nearby enemies.
- The timer counts up, and your size is shown in the top-left corner.

## Controls
**Mobile (Android):**
- Drag the on-screen joystick (bottom-left). The donut faces the direction you push and moves faster the further you push.
- Tap the pause button (top-right) to pause. The Android back button also pauses.

**Desktop (for testing):**
- Move the mouse to aim, hold W to move forward and S to reverse.
- Esc pauses.

## Install on Android
1. Download `MobileBuild/Level_Fortress.apk` to your phone.
2. Open it. If asked, allow installing apps from this source.
3. Tap Install, then open the game.

## Features
- Tilemap level with multiple walled buildings and doorways of different sizes
- Spawning food and enemies sized relative to the player
- Sound effects, background music, particles, animations and screen shake
- Main menu, pause menu and game over screen with fade transitions
- Touch controls for mobile
